The review covers scientific validity, modelling practice, beginner progression, assessment quality, software integrity, accessibility and horizontal curriculum promises.

## Scientific gates passed

- Random validation is presented as an interpolation comparator, not evidence of new-site or new-region transfer.
- Grouping fields are selected from the intended prediction destination, then audited rather than trusted by function name.
- Site-level, leave-one-site-out, spatial-block and buffered designs are distinguished accurately.
- Buffer distance requires appropriate coordinates, a prior rationale and a sample-impact audit.
- Temporal folds respect past-to-future direction and audit predictor availability, not merely target-row dates.
- Phenology, management, sensor/processing and environmental drift remain separate hypotheses.
- Nested validation keeps preprocessing and model selection inside outer development partitions.
- Final-test evidence remains sealed.
- All Chapter 3 tabular values, sites, coordinates, dates and target measurements are labelled synthetic.

## Deliberate limits

- Four synthetic sites and three years demonstrate evidence architecture but cannot support a real coastal-meadow transfer claim.
- No block arrangement establishes another-region performance without independent regional evidence.
- Chapter 3 uses only a tiny candidate set to teach nesting. It is not the hyperparameter-optimisation chapter.
- Full regression/classification diagnostics, applicability mapping and uncertainty remain assigned to Chapters 5–6.
- Buffering is taught as a concept and audit; the synthetic coordinate grid is not a real CRS.

## Release decision

Release Chapter 3 as the validation standard for Module 3. Chapter 4 may proceed only if every optimisation remains inside the structured outer/inner architecture established here and the final test stays sealed.
