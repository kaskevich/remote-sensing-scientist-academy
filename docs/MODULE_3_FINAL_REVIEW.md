# Module 3 final multi-lens review

**Review date:** 18 August 2026

**Scope:** Lessons 3.1–3.30, seven chapter packs, Environmental Monitoring Project capstone, learner workflow, mobile presentation and release evidence

**Decision:** **Publish as the completed Remote Sensing Modelling module, with explicit limits on synthetic teaching evidence.**

## Executive judgement

Module 3 now delivers the homepage promise within a bounded educational scope. A learner progresses from prediction framing through target and predictor contracts, baseline and XGBoost reasoning, spatial/temporal/nested validation, controlled optimisation, regression and classification evaluation, residual geography, interpretation boundaries, applicability, uncertainty, raster inference, Earth Engine architecture, repeated monitoring and operational model governance. The capstone requires the learner to combine those capabilities independently and can refuse release when evidence is inadequate.

The strongest feature is the claim–evidence discipline: target meaning, prediction unit, training roles, protected evaluation, uncertainty and applicability remain separate and reviewable. The most important limitation is equally explicit: Academy fixtures are synthetic. They demonstrate methods and failure controls but cannot establish real coastal-meadow accuracy, uncertainty, operational readiness or ecological change.

## Horizontal curriculum integrity

Module 3 consumes rather than repeats Module 1 Python and Module 2 geospatial processing. The capstone requires earlier raster, spatial-support, QA and reproducible-delivery capabilities but assesses genuinely new predictive judgement. It does not advertise deep learning, causal inference or live production deployment as completed competencies.

The three graduate profiles are evidenced as follows:

- **GIS / Remote Sensing Engineer:** prediction schema, raster/window invariants, NoData/grid QA, cloud/local interface and reproducible operations;
- **Geospatial Data Analyst:** target/metric reasoning, baseline, structured evaluation, residual geography and decision communication;
- **Remote Sensing Researcher:** measurement hypotheses, sampling and transfer logic, protected evidence, uncertainty, applicability and bounded scientific interpretation.

One capstone may support overlapping profiles only when the submitted evidence matrix points to inspectable artifacts. The module does not guarantee employment or claim that every graduate has identical depth in all three profiles.

## Release conditions

Publication is approved when automated unit, content, manifest, type, lint, build and browser checks pass. The live page must expose all thirty lessons plus the available capstone, retain completion/notes/uploads/submissions, open the capstone by direct link, and avoid horizontal overflow at 320 px, 375 px, 768 px and desktop widths.

Future revisions should update official software links and tested versions, maintain the synthetic-evidence warning, and add real case studies only when licence, metadata, measurement protocol, geographic privacy and independent validation are adequate.
