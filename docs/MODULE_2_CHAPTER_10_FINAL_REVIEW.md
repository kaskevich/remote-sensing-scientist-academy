# Module 2 Chapter 10 final review

Review date: 11 August 2026  
Scope: Lesson 2.46, Practicum 10, training pack, portfolio integration and automated curriculum coverage

## Educational decision

Chapter 10 contains one extended professional lesson because its purpose is synthesis, not a new sequence of product tutorials. The lesson establishes the conceptual framework; the practicum supplies the substantial independent architecture, governance, equivalence and migration work.

The chapter does not require a paid ArcGIS licence. It teaches credible professional roles and verification methods while preventing learners from inventing product evidence when they lack authorised access.

## Vertical lesson quality

Lesson 2.46 includes:

- one central concept: implementations can change while scientific invariants remain fixed;
- accurate bounded roles for ArcGIS Pro, geodatabases, ModelBuilder, ArcPy, ArcGIS Online and ArcGIS Enterprise;
- fair comparison with QGIS, GeoPandas, Rasterio, PostGIS and standards-based delivery;
- explicit distinction between visual orchestration, executable evidence and scientific reproducibility;
- authority, public-sharing, licence/privilege, operations, recovery and exit reasoning;
- a worked comparison gate, prediction, line-by-line explanation, common mistakes, guided practice, independent challenge, scientific interpretation and portfolio submission;
- three formative checks aligned to the lesson concept.

## Horizontal curriculum integrity

Chapter 10 consumes evidence from:

- Chapter 7 authorities, roles, constraints and restore evidence;
- Chapter 8 immutable COG/STAC assets and cloud-native access contracts;
- Chapter 9 public-schema, accessible map/table and interoperability tests.

It adds **Artifact 2.J — Portable Professional GIS Architecture** to the same UAV and Satellite Analysis Pipeline. It does not create an unrelated product exercise.

## Training evidence

The CC0 synthetic pack contains:

- ten workflow requirements spanning scientific, user, security, offline and recovery needs;
- twelve candidate components with deliberately incomplete capability claims;
- ten stage-by-stage ArcGIS/open workflow translations;
- ten public, organisational and interoperability sharing risks;
- three contrasting operating environments;
- a complete professional ecosystem QA template;
- checksums and byte counts for every supplied evidence file.

No real organisation, account, licence, credential, endpoint or sensitive location is represented.

## Intentional limits

The chapter does not:

- teach every ArcGIS interface or administration task;
- state current product prices, credits, extensions or entitlements;
- claim a live ArcGIS deployment was tested;
- require purchase or sign-up;
- declare ArcGIS or open-source software universally superior;
- claim that opening an exported file proves semantic or scientific equivalence;
- expose credentials, private endpoints or architecture secrets.

These limits keep the chapter scientifically focused, licence-neutral and maintainable.
