Review date: 18 August 2026  
Scope: Lessons 3.13–3.16, Chapter 4 training pack, continuous notebook, runtime contracts and browser integration

## Outcome

Chapter 4 is ready for publication as the controlled model-selection chapter. It preserves Chapter 3’s nested evidence firewall and teaches four decisions separately: hyperparameter search, stopping and regularisation, feature-schema stability, and imbalanced-class thresholding. It does not present optimisation as a route to guaranteed improvement.

## Scientific integrity gates passed

- Random search occurs only inside the current outer development partition.
- Inner folds retain spatial groups; ordinary random K-fold is not silently substituted.
- Outer assessment evaluates the complete selection procedure and cannot choose a candidate.
- The final test cannot choose parameters, stopping rounds, features, weights or thresholds.
- Early-stopping `eval_set` is explicitly development evidence.
- Permutation importance is described as model-, metric- and dataset-dependent predictive reliance, not causality.
- Correlated predictors are not removed by an automatic universal threshold.
- Rare-habitat metrics name the positive class and preserve confusion counts.
- Class weighting and sampling remain inside training roles.
- A 0.5 threshold is presented as an API default, not a scientific law.

## Content limitations retained deliberately

Chapter 4 is **not the complete evaluation and interpretation chapter**. Calibration, full regression diagnostics, residual geography, SHAP, applicability and extrapolation are reserved for Chapter 5. The threshold lesson introduces probability-quality caution but does not claim to complete calibration analysis.

All numeric teaching fixtures are synthetic. They cannot support a real coastal-meadow transfer claim, a real habitat prevalence estimate or a production threshold. Scientific context is informed by the cited Baltic coastal plant-traits record, but the Academy does not invent undocumented units, community-code meanings or sampling claims.

## Release recommendation

Publish Lessons 3.13–3.16 together. Their shared artifact—the **Controlled Model Selection Record**—depends on the complete sequence. Keep Lessons 3.17–3.30 and the capstone visibly planned until their independent reviews pass.
