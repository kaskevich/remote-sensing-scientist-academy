# Species image visual-quality audit

Audited 2026-09-04. All 78 primary Atlas card images were reviewed in labelled contact sheets. The review preferred a whole living plant, then a flowering or fruiting plant, then a clear diagnostic close-up. Licensing, creator and source metadata remain mandatory independently of visual quality.

## Outcome

- Cards audited: 78
- Remaining placeholder cards: 0
- Primary images replaced after visual review: 25
- Taxa with no suitable reusable image requiring manual review: none
- Full reusable inventories additionally reviewed: *Prunella vulgaris*, *Arrhenatherum elatius* and *Trifolium pratense*

The previous *Phragmites australis* primary showed dead/rhizome material. It was replaced with FinBIF media `MM.122065`, which shows living reed foliage and inflorescences.
