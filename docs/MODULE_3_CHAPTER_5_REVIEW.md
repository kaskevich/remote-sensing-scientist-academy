# Module 3 Chapter 5 quality review

Review date: 18 August 2026
Scope: Lessons 3.17–3.21, evaluation/applicability pack, continuous notebook, runtime contracts and browser integration

## Outcome

Chapter 5 is ready for publication as the evaluation, diagnosis and applicability chapter. It uses only fixed protected predictions and keeps five questions distinct: regression error, classification/probability quality, structured failure, predictive interpretation and environmental support. Its signature artifact prevents a visually complete prediction map from implying equally strong evidence everywhere.

Overall multi-lens score: **4.82 / 5**

## Scientific integrity gates passed

- MAE, RMSE, bias and R² are interpreted together and retain fold-level counts and spread.
- Regression residual sign and target units are explicit.
- Classification decisions, ranking and calibration are separate evidence questions.
- ROC is not called invalid under imbalance; the operational region, PR evidence, prevalence and counts are retained.
- Calibration is treated as learned model development and cannot fit on the assessment it reports.
- Residual maps use trusted one-to-one joins and do not infer mechanisms from spatial patterns.
- Post-hoc subgroup findings are labelled exploratory and require new evidence before model revision.
- Gain, permutation, PDP/ICE and SHAP are compared by question and limitation.
- Feature importance and SHAP are never presented as causal ecological mechanisms.
- Applicability scaling and thresholds use development evidence consistent with validation.
- Geographic proximity, univariate ranges and multivariate environmental support remain distinct.
- NoData, failed QA, review and outside-applicability states remain distinct and machine-readable.
- The final test stays sealed.

## Content limitations retained deliberately

Chapter 5 is **not the prediction-uncertainty chapter**. Fold variability, calibration and applicability qualify performance, but they are not individual prediction intervals. Measurement, sampling, model, residual, transfer and extrapolation uncertainty are reserved for Chapter 6.

All numeric teaching fixtures and generalized coordinates are synthetic. They cannot support a real vegetation-height accuracy claim, habitat prevalence estimate, ecological mechanism, operational probability or Baltic Domain of Applicability. The published dataset provides scientific context only; undocumented units and community-code meanings are not invented.

## Release recommendation

Publish Lessons 3.17–3.21 together. Their shared artifact—the **Model Diagnostic and Applicability Package**—depends on the complete sequence. At the Chapter 5 release gate, Lessons 3.22–3.30 and the capstone remained planned pending independent review; Chapter 6 has since passed that review, while Chapter 7 and the capstone remain planned.
